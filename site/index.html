Qwen3.8-27B · one Swift binary · Apple silicon

Your Mac, now a local AI server.

Strim runs Qwen3.8-27B as a local backend for Claude Code and Codex. Nothing leaves the machine.

$ brew install megasoft1978/strim/strim
$ brew install --cask megasoft1978/strim/strim-app
$ strim pull

Installs the strim command. The menu-bar app (second line) is optional; it already contains the CLI.

$ curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/megasoft1978/strim-engine/main/packaging/install.sh | bash
$ strim pull

No sudo. Installs to ~/.local/strim and links ~/.local/bin/strim. Checks the SHA-256 before installing.

Download the DMG (arm64)Open the DMG. Drag Strim to Applications. Launch Strim from the menu bar.

Not notarized yet: right-click Open. macOS warns on first launch. Right-click Strim in Applications and choose Open, or click Open Anyway in System Settings, Privacy and Security, or run xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Strim.app. Homebrew avoids the warning.

Model weights (about 16 GB) come separately with strim pull. Apple Silicon, macOS 14 or newer, tested on a 64 GB M5 Max. MIT.

FAQ

Questions.

Why only one model?

Everything is tuned for one dense model: its attention state, its speculative-decoding head and its prefix cache. That is why these numbers are possible, and why Strim will not run your other models. Others are on the roadmap.

Does it run on a 32 GB Mac or an M1?

Only a 64 GB M5 Max has been tested. For 32 GB the computed estimate is 114K/214K (low) 154K/291K (high) of context (not measured); 16 GB and below are unsupported. See supported scope.

Does speculative decoding change the output?

Draft tokens are verified by the full model, but batching can flip near-tie tokens. Greedy output over 8 fixed prompts and 64 tokens: Strim against itself 8/8 identical, against rapid-mlx 7/8, against mlx-vlm 6/8, against mlx-lm 6/8, against oMLX 7/8. Quality and exactness.
