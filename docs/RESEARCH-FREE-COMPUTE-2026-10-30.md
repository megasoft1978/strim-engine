# Free compute for Strim: training, evaluation and hardware access

Research snapshot: 30 October 2026. This extends [the first pass](RESEARCH-HARDWARE-ACCESS-2026-10-30.md); its Mac loans, CI hosts and baseline visibility advice remain relevant. Links below are primary program or product pages unless otherwise identified. Offers, quotas, model support and deadlines can change; recheck before applying. No applications have been submitted.

**Fit** is a judgment for an independent Italian developer with the stated assets, not an award probability: 5 = direct, strong use; 1 = speculative or gated. “Rolling” means no published deadline found, not a promise that applications are open indefinitely. “Verify” means a current offer, eligibility, terms, or deadline was not confirmed. An API inference allowance, a hosted fine-tune, cash, a loan and unrestricted GPU hours are different things. Linux GPU/TPU compute can train or evaluate separate model pipelines; it does not run Swift/MLX Metal as on a Mac.

## Training feasibility and reusable package

1. **Protect the evaluation.** Separate training, validation and held-out tasks by repository/problem family, prevent reference solutions and hidden tests reaching the agent, and test the verifier against false positives, timeout and resource exhaustion. A public example verifier does not require publishing every held-out test. Verify licenses and rights to source code and recorded trajectories before releasing or uploading them.
2. **Create one reusable artifact.** Aim for 10–20 tasks across React/Node/TypeScript, Go, Ruby and Python as a practical initial target, not a provider requirement. Document task counts, reproducible sandbox, pass/fail reward, deterministic reset, language mix, difficulty, baseline pass@1 and limitations. Adapt it separately to OpenEnv and Prime verifiers; their APIs and admission checks are not interchangeable.
3. **Budget three distinct experiments.** (a) SFT/LoRA on successful, sanitized trajectories; (b) GRPO or another verifiable-reward method with sandbox rollouts; (c) draft-head or quantization distillation only where the training stack allows custom heads/losses and compatible checkpoint export. Estimate VRAM, context length, rollout tokens, GPU-hours, sandbox CPU, storage and transfer; trial a small model before a 27B run. Vendor API credits may not pay for all of these. Confirm model and adapter licensing, formats, and conversion to MLX before declaring the result deployable.
4. **Make results credible.** Compare base, SFT and RL under equal evaluation budget on held-out tasks; report confidence intervals, seed count, compute, failures and contamination limitations. Publish a short technical report, scripts and model/data cards where terms permit.

## Ranked 30/60/90-day plan

### Days 1–30: build evidence and make direct applications

1. **OpenEnv Arena (fit 5):** select 10–20 tasks and implement the required OpenEnv endpoints; validate finite rewards and image constraints, then submit to the live challenge after checking its rules and deadline. Preserve sealed tests and record admission/training outcome.
2. **Prime bounty (5):** inspect live bounty list, claim an actual open task first, adapt a representative environment to verifiers, and submit for review. Confirm cash/credit terms before relying on them.
3. **Tinker grant (5):** prepare a two-page SFT-versus-RL proposal for Qwen3.8-27B, a dataset sample and token budget; ask about independent Italy eligibility, adapter export and grant scope, then apply via the research form.
4. **Lambda (5):** reuse the research plan with GPU-hours and checkpointed runs; submit the rolling research application. Use the up-to-$5k figure as a ceiling, not an expected award.
5. **University partner (5):** approach suitable Italian ML/systems labs with a concrete co-authored problem and contribution; agree data, IP, authorship and allocation process. Do not assume a partner grants access automatically.
6. **Nebius (4):** check whether its 2026–27 research call still accepts applications; submit if open and eligible. Independently evaluate the AI Builder program's actual training-credit coverage.
7. **NLnet (3):** Before 3 November 2026 noon CET, check Restack and CodeSupply fit and the AI policy; only apply if the human-authored proposal and intended costs qualify.
8. **Small free tiers (3):** prepare CPU-first portable notebooks, then use Kaggle/Lightning/Modal only within current quotas and spend limits to validate small-scale code. These are not a substitute for a 27B training allocation.

### Days 31–60: turn artifacts into allocation proposals

9. **Publish the benchmark and report (5):** release the environment, a short methodology report, baseline results, model/data cards and a public demo Space. Make one careful public announcement and invite corrections.
10. **Academic allocation (4):** with an engaged qualified PI, submit an appropriately sized CINECA try/Class C or AI-for-science proposal; prepare NVIDIA Academic or Google Cloud proposals if the collaboration and current call qualify. Start with measured scalability, not a speculative large request.
11. **Industry route (3–4):** contact IT4LIA and check EuroHPC industrial eligibility; apply only if there is an eligible organization and PI. Use the correct track and its live deadline; 13 November and 30 November 2026 are listed large-scale cutoffs, not universal deadlines.
12. **Sponsorship (3–4):** send a bounded evaluation proposal to Mac hosts and relevant hardware vendors using published results; offer objective reporting and disclose support. Launch a transparent compute fund and approach prospective corporate users with a specific deliverable.

### Days 61–90: run and publish

13. **Run the smallest funded comparison (5):** if credits or an allocation are awarded, train SFT and RL baselines, monitor actual spend, and retain checkpoints only where allowed. If no award, publish CPU/small-model verifier evidence and continue applications; do not claim 27B results.
14. **Assess distillation (4):** confirm access to custom loss/head training and export, then prototype MTP or quantization distillation on compatible resources. Publish a success or failure report with measured quality and performance tradeoffs.
15. **Extend based on evidence (3):** pursue follow-on bounties, active hackathons, research partnerships, or an eligible NLnet/Open Internet Stack call; verify program status, any AI policy, allowed costs and deadlines afresh.
